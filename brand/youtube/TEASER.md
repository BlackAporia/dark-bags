## Important: YouTube's rules on stakes

YouTube does not allow videos that send viewers to gambling sites it has not reviewed. That is why the description puts the free game first. Stakes are only "optional, 18+, where legal", and the video and text never promise winnings.
- Do not add "win money", "earn", "play to earn" or "real payouts" to the title, the description or the comments.
- The $ amounts in the teaser are an in-game result screen. Do not add captions that turn them into income.
