    How a raid works
    • Stake. 5% rake. Half of the rest rides in your bag, half is scattered on the map as loot.
    • Loot. Orange dust, stacks, and vault chests. Dropped bags show a ? until someone opens them.
    • Bluff. Your bag's look is a choice (Q). Look broke and slip out, or look fat and bait a fight.
    • Extract. Stand in a green exit for 3 seconds. You cash out whatever you carry.
