    How a raid works
    • Ready room. Stake and press Ready. Your icon lights up; the countdown starts; everyone drops in together.
    • Arms race. Knife → pistol → shotgun → SMG → rifle → laser sniper → knife again. Kills move you up; damage and loot add a little.
    • Storm. The circle shrinks four times and collapses onto one exit. Exits it swallows close for good.
    • Bluff. Your bag's look is a choice (Q). Look broke and slip out, or look fat and bait a fight.
    • Extract. Stand in an open exit for 3 seconds. You cash out whatever you carry. Still inside at 0:00, you lose it.
