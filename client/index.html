    How to play
    1. Stake and ready up. Everyone drops in together with a knife and 100 health. Half your stake rides in your bag, half is scattered as loot.
    2. Grab the orange loot. It goes into your bag. Kills upgrade your weapon: knife → pistol → shotgun → SMG → rifle → laser sniper.
    3. Get out alive. Stand in a green exit for 3 seconds before the storm closes it. You keep your whole bag. Die and it drops for someone else.
