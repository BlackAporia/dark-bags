Built on Starknet

Private by default. Proven on chain.

Starknet's zero-knowledge stack does what a game with real money needs: nobody sees what you hold, what you stake or what you win. Everything below is live in the game today.

🛡️

Private deposits

Top up from your shielded balance through the STRK20 privacy pool. A zero-knowledge proof checks it; nobody on chain sees who paid or how much.

🎲

Private stakes

Pick any stake in a table's range. Only you know it: the table shows only the whole pool, and prizes split fairly, like poker side pots.

🔁

Private swaps

Swap coins inside your game balance at the live price. It never touches the chain, so no explorer can show it.

🏦

Private cash-outs

Winnings go back into your shielded balance through STRK20: nobody on chain sees the payout was yours.

One raid, three minutes

In with a bag. Out with more. Or not at all.

  1. 01

    Stake your bag

    Pick a mode and a table. Your stake goes in your bag, and everyone can see roughly how full it looks. Bluff it lighter or heavier.

  2. 02

    Fight up the ladder

    Everyone starts with a knife. Every kill levels your weapon: pistol, shotgun, SMG, rifle, sniper. Drop a runner and their bag is yours to grab.

  3. 03

    Extract before the storm

    The one exit sits in the middle of the map. Hold it for 10 seconds while the whole raid is told you are leaving, and everything in your bag is yours. Get hit and the timer resets.

Questions

Before you drop in

Is it free?

Yes. Practice mode against bots needs nothing at all, not even an account. Online play on the testnet uses free test tokens.

Do I need crypto to play?

No. You can play practice right away. For online testnet play, sign in with a Starknet wallet or with email and grab test tokens from a faucet.

Is there real money in it right now?

Not yet. The public test runs on Starknet Sepolia, where tokens have no value. Real-token play comes later as a closed beta, only for adults and only where it is legal.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in the browser. Turn the phone sideways: move with the left stick, hold Fire on the right.

Can people cheat?

The server runs the raid and checks every move, hit and payout. The game in your browser only draws what the server says happened.
